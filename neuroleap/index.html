Neuroleap

Accessible guidance for a healthcare experience

Discipline
Content design · Accessibility
Role
UX Writer / Designer
Year
2023
Product
Digital health

The challenge

Healthcare experiences ask people to process unfamiliar information when attention and confidence may already be limited. Neuroleap needed language that felt credible without becoming clinical, and supportive without hiding important detail.

Designing for comprehension

I applied plain-language and progressive-disclosure principles to onboarding, task instructions, and feedback. Headings surfaced the decision first; supporting details followed only when needed.

Clarity is an accessibility feature: people should not need specialist vocabulary to understand what the product is asking.

A consistent voice

I defined a calm, direct content pattern across prompts, confirmations, and errors. Error messages described what happened, preserved user agency, and offered a concrete recovery step.

  • Front-load the action.
  • Use familiar words and short sentences.
  • Never blame the user.
  • Pair reassurance with useful next steps.
