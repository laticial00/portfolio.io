Iron Mountain

Making a technical 3D workflow teachable

Discipline
Technical writing · UX research
Role
UX Writer / Research Intern
Year
2022
Product
Enterprise asset management

The challenge

Users needed to move 3D assets from SketchUp to Sketchfab and publish them in WordPress. The workflow crossed tools, formats, and permission boundaries—and existing knowledge lived with individual experts.

From expert steps to user tasks

I interviewed stakeholders, reproduced the workflow, and reorganized it around user decisions instead of software menus. The guide paired concise instructions with screenshots, prerequisites, troubleshooting, and a companion video.

Each step answered three questions: What do I do? What should I see? What if it fails?

A reusable handoff

I created step-by-step onboarding and WordPress instructions that let new contributors complete the workflow independently. Content was structured for scanning and reuse, with consistent action-led headings and visible checkpoints.
