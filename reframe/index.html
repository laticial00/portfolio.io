Reframe

Turning behavioral science into supportive action

Discipline
UX writing · Editorial
Role
Content Writer
Year
2023
Product
Behavior-change platform

The challenge

Reframe translates the science of alcohol use and habit change into everyday guidance. The content needed to remain accurate while meeting readers with empathy—not judgment.

Science people can use

I translated concepts including dopamine response and the hedonic set point into an accessible narrative. Instead of flattening the research, I layered it: a clear takeaway first, a plain-language explanation second, and practical reflection last.

Tone as product strategy

I used second-person language selectively, avoided absolutes, and framed behavior change as a series of possible next steps. The result balanced authority with warmth and made dense health information easier to act on.

The voice didn’t promise a transformation. It helped the reader recognize one achievable next move.
