Nuzzly

Marketplace clarity from discovery to booking

Discipline
UX writing · Product strategy
Role
QA + UX Content
Year
2026
Product
Pet-care marketplace

The challenge

Nuzzly connected pet owners with trusted sitters, but unclear labels, disconnected states, and missing guidance made core tasks feel uncertain. I audited the live experience through hands-on QA and session replay, then prioritized language changes with the highest impact on trust and task completion.

The content problem wasn’t “better wording.” It was making the product’s state visible at every step.

01 · Explore

Problem

Search results, dates, and empty states did not consistently explain what had happened or what someone could do next.

Content direction

I defined clearer discovery language, validation behavior, and recovery paths. Empty results shifted from a dead end to a useful next step: adjust dates, expand location, or explore available sitters.

02 · Messages

Problem

Booking status and message actions diverged across screens. Duplicate actions and ambiguous status changes undermined confidence.

Content direction

I mapped messages to the booking lifecycle, removed competing labels, and wrote status-specific guidance so owners and sitters could understand what changed—and what came next.

03 · Dashboard

Problem

Hosts lacked a reliable path to review, update, and understand their listings.

Content direction

I reorganized dashboard language around user goals, clarified listing states, and specified actionable empty states and confirmations.
