Overview

Designing trust across a two-sided marketplace.

Nuzzly coordinates owners, sitters, verification, listings, applications, messages, bookings, and private contact information. I traced complete journeys, documented friction with screenshots and reproduction steps, and paired content recommendations with interface behavior and QA criteria.

01

LISTINGS + MARKETPLACE DISCOVERY

Make the active task and search state visible.

Explore served owners looking for sitters and sitters looking for listings. Competing controls, overlapping save actions, and hidden filter states made the current mode difficult to read.

02

DIRECT MESSAGES + BOOKING STATE

Make every surface tell the same story.

Messages carried applications, acceptances, confirmations, cancellations, and private-information rules. Small wording inconsistencies could change what users believed had happened.

A

Cancellation clarity

Two actions used “Cancel” even though one dismissed the dialog and the other cancelled the booking.

B

Booking-state consistency

The timeline said the sitter declined while the booking card still said “Booking Confirmed.”

03

LISTING MANAGEMENT + DASHBOARDS

Organize each role around its next decision.

The sitter dashboard mixed confirmed-booking content, technical placeholder language, and duplicate Message actions. Owners also needed a visible “Add trip” action and direct access to applications, unread messages, and reviews.

Missing actionAdd trip

Surface the owner’s frequent next task.

Misleading empty state“Right now,” not “yet”

“You don’t have any active sits right now” does not erase past history.

Hidden urgencyVisible counts

Bring applications, messages, and pending reviews into the overview.

Outcome

From isolated strings to a testable content system.

I delivered a prioritized UX and content audit, annotated evidence, flow documentation, interface recommendations, and reusable checks for statuses, actions, permissions, safety, and recovery.
