Overview

Trust depended on knowing exactly what happens next.

Nuzzly coordinates pet owners, sitters, homes, payments, and private information. I tested complete journeys—not isolated screens—and found places where content, system state, and available actions contradicted one another.

My work connected more than 40 findings to clearer interface language, product recommendations, and testable launch criteria.

What I contributed

  • Owner and sitter journey audits
  • Microcopy and status language
  • Annotated recommendations
  • Regression and launch criteria
  • PostHog session review

+

SUPPORTING EXPLORATIONS

The same principles, across the product.

Sign-in“Last used”

Recognize the remembered method and offer “Use another email.”

Empty states“Right now”

Avoid “yet” when someone may already have past sits.

Community“Be the first to like”

Invite participation without inventing activity.

Reviews“Needs answers”

Show what requires attention without blame.
